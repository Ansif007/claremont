About us

Helping students make bold academic moves with clarity.

Claremont INC is an overseas education consultancy focused on practical advice, careful planning, and dependable support through every stage of the student journey.

Our story

We built Claremont INC around a simple belief: overseas education decisions should feel informed, not confusing. Students deserve guidance that balances ambition with realistic pathways, financial planning, and visa readiness.

Our team supports students aiming for established global destinations and helps them move from exploration to enrollment with more confidence and less uncertainty.

What students can expect

  • Honest, profile-based counseling
  • Structured application planning
  • Responsive support across milestones
  • Guidance that stays practical and personalized
